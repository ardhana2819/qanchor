Visual directions, polish round

Three directions inside "utilitarian + polish". Each panel is live: press Generate (results stagger in), copy a row (icon morphs into a check), switch tabs (indicator slides), focus the form (panel lifts). Reduced motion turns every transition off.

A · Anchor, refinedChosen

The approved v0.3 look, plus a polish layer.

  • Mood: calm, precise, quietly crafted. Depth from 1 px highlights and soft elevation, not colour.
  • Principles: data first, motion explains a change, one accent.
  • Palette: cool neutrals, teal #0F766E / #2DD4BF (unchanged).
  • Fonts: Inter + system mono. +0 KB; tokens from T-005 stay.
  • Like: Linear, Vercel dashboard.
  • Why: keeps every approved decision and finished task; the new rules are met through motion and depth only.

B · Tidewater

Deeper, softer, Raycast-like panels.

  • Mood: immersive, tactile. Larger radii, layered shadows, panels that lift on focus.
  • Principles: panels as objects, tactile feedback, mono gives data its identity.
  • Palette: blue-slate neutrals, cyan-teal #0E7490 / #5EEAD4, orange for errors.
  • Fonts: Inter + JetBrains Mono. About +40 KB woff2 (estimate, to be measured), off the critical path.
  • Like: Raycast, Linear (dark).
  • Why: the strongest "delightful" feel for interviewers. Costs: new tokens, a second font, and T-005 rework.

C · Ledger ink

Warm paper, ink-blue accent, Arc-like colour coding.

  • Mood: editorial, warm, human. Category colour dots help you find tools.
  • Principles: warmth over chrome, colour encodes category, typography carries hierarchy.
  • Palette: warm stone neutrals, ink blue #1D4ED8 / #8AB4FF, teal and amber category dots.
  • Fonts: Geist + Geist Mono, replacing Inter. About +25 KB net (estimate, to be measured).
  • Like: Arc, Vercel (Geist).
  • Why: most distinctive among QA tools. Costs: a palette and font switch, T-005 rework, and blue that is less ownable than teal.
